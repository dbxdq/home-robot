# Research note — 2026-11-17 (kickoff)

The question that started this repo: *I want a personal robot at home,
but the market has no unified interface — why can't it be like building
a PC?*

## What exists now (verified 2026-11-17)

**Software has standards; hardware does not.**
- ROS 2 (Jazzy LTS) is the middleware: Nav2, MoveIt 2, Gazebo. The
  closest thing to a robot OS.
- Hugging Face LeRobot is the closest thing to an application layer:
  teleoperate → record → train → deploy, open models and datasets.
  11,788 SO-101 datasets on the Hub (Nov 2026).

**The modular parts.**
- SO-101 arm: 6-DOF, 3D-printed, Feetech STS3215 servos. Official BOM:
  $229.88 for a leader+follower pair in the US, ¥1,343 in China
  (single follower $121.94 / ¥682). Assembled kits $199–$299
  (WowRobo, Seeed, Hiwonder). Payload ~200–500 g.
- Unitree Go2 Air quadruped: from $1,600 in the US; ¥8,997 on JD,
  ¥7,647 after the national subsidy. ROS 2 supported. A mobile base
  for later.
- Orbbec Gemini 335 depth camera: $264 overseas, ¥1,950 in China.
  ROS 2 supported, the direct RealSense replacement.
- Compute has no domestic clone: Jetson Orin NX or a laptop.

**The one buyable home humanoid.**
- 1X NEO: $20,000 early-access purchase or $499/month, $200 refundable
  deposit, US deliveries starting 2026. Basic autonomy at launch;
  Expert Mode (remote human operator) for chores it cannot do alone.
  Laundry and folding are listed as tasks, with the same remote-help
  caveat. No independently verified home use as of Nov 2026.
- Hello Robot Stretch 4 ($29,950) is the research-grade alternative:
  wheeled mobile manipulator, ROS 2 + Python, self-charging, 2.5 kg
  extended payload — a developer platform, not a consumer appliance.

## Why no unified platform

1. No standard body — every task wants different hardware.
2. No volume — thousands of units a year, not hundreds of millions.
3. Safety and liability — a wrong move breaks things or hurts people,
   so vendors stay closed.
4. The software layer is newborn — VLA models only took off after 2024;
   there has been no "Windows moment" yet.
5. Attempts failed: Willow Garage PR2 (shut 2014), Google Everyday
   Robots (cut 2023), Amazon Astro (closed product, not a platform).

Two things are changing: the humanoid is becoming the default body
(homes are built for human bodies), and VLA models may become the
unifying layer (one model, many bodies).

## Decision

- Not buying NEO now: early access, unproven at home, $20,000.
- Build path: first skill is "pick up scattered objects, put them in
  a bin" — SO-101 desktop arm to start, mobile base later.
- The game project comes first; this repo runs as background daily
  research in the meantime.
