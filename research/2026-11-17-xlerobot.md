# Research note — 2026-11-17 (XLeRobot)

User flagged this one the same day the repo was created: "xle这个推车机器人不错".

## What it is

XLeRobot is an open-source dual-arm mobile home robot by Gaotian
"Vector" Wang (Rice CS PhD student): LeRobot + two SO-101 arms bolted
onto a $40 IKEA RÅSKOG utility cart. Apache-2.0, ~5.6K GitHub stars
(Nov 2026).

## Why it matters for this project

It is the "build" path in one box. Instead of SO-101 on a desk first
and a Go2 base later, XLeRobot combines both for roughly $700 total:

| Part | Price |
| --- | --- |
| IKEA RÅSKOG cart (3-tier, the frame) | $40 |
| Everything else (2× SO-101 arms, 17 Feetech STS3215 servos, omni wheels, power station, cameras) | ~$660 |

Your laptop is the brain (BYO compute over Wi-Fi); a 288 Wh camping
power station gives ~10 hours. Teleoperation via Joy-Con / Xbox
controller / keyboard, or record demos and train a LeRobot policy.
VR Quest 3 simulation support. Claimed assembly under 4 hours.
Developer assembly kit on sale since 2025-09-09.

## What it has been shown doing

- Autonomously: get a drink from the fridge, fill a cup with ice.
- Teleoperated: navigate a living room, wipe a countertop, pick up
  laundry, water plants.

Note the split: the autonomous demos are the easy, fixed-scene ones;
the housework demos are teleoperated. That is the honest state of
the art at this price.

## Limits (from the docs and independent write-ups)

- Not designed for in-hand dexterity, lifting over 1 kg per arm, or
  dynamic movements.
- Needs flat floors to drive.
- No onboard computer in the basic build; no IMU, lidar, odometry,
  or battery data link; no shipped policy — you record your own demos.
- 17 servos, 15 DoF total, ~1.20 m tall.

## Verdict

The cheapest credible mobile-manipulation platform for the home, and
the most direct next step for this repo's build path: if the SO-101
desk stage works, XLeRobot is the assembled form of it, not a
separate project. The missing piece is still the same — a policy that
does a chore reliably without a human driving.
